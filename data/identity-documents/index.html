Clean and scanned

Every document also comes as a scanned copy: tilted, toned, noisy and slightly blurred, with its labels moved to match.

The full set

  • Document types

    ID cards, passports and driving licences.

  • Fictional countries

    Utopia, Waha, Severia and more: realistic layouts that copy no real government's design.

  • Generated portraits

    Faces in the full set are generated. No real person appears.

  • Working MRZ

    Machine-readable zones follow ICAO 9303, with correct check digits.

  • Always a specimen

    Every document carries a SPECIMEN mark, labelled like any other field.

  • Scan and photo variants

    Tilt, glare, blur and crops, as if photographed on a phone.
