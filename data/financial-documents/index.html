Clean and scanned

Every document also comes as a scanned copy: tilted, toned, noisy and slightly blurred, with its labels moved to match.

The full set

  • Document types

    Bank statements, invoices, receipts, utility bills and wire transfers.

  • More than 100 languages

    Including right-to-left scripts and Chinese, Japanese and Korean, rendered correctly.

  • Many fictional institutions

    Each with its own name, branding and layout, so models don't learn one look.

  • Numbers that add up

    Balances, totals and tax are calculated, not invented.

    Tilt, paper tone, noise and blur, at the strength you choose.
