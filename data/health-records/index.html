Clean and scanned

Every document also comes as a scanned copy: tilted, toned, noisy and slightly blurred, with its labels moved to match.

The full set

  • Document types

    Lab reports, prescriptions, discharge summaries and insurance claims.

  • More than 100 languages

    Including Hindi, Arabic and other scripts that OCR often gets wrong.

  • Plausible values

    Test results with units, reference ranges and high and low flags.

  • No real patients

    Every person, clinic and number is generated. Nothing comes from medical records.

  • Scan variants

    Tilt, paper tone, noise and blur, at the strength you choose.

  • Custom batches

    Your document types, fields, languages and volume.
