Lien

Draw and analyse networks: people, companies, accounts and the links between them. Free to use online or download.

Open the canvas, free →

What you can do

  • Draw

    Double-click to add a node. Connect two nodes, name them, colour them by type.

  • Import

    Paste or load a CSV of links (source, target, label) or a JSON graph.

  • Lay out

    Force-directed, circle, grid and concentric layouts, in one click.

  • Find what matters

    Rank nodes by connections and by how often they sit between others.

  • Export

    Save the graph as JSON to pick up later, or as a PNG image.

  • Private

    Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Bring your own data

A CSV with one link per row is enough. Lien creates the nodes for you.
