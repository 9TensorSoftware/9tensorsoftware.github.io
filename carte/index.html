Carte

Open KML, KMZ, GeoJSON and GPX files on a map, layer them and explore. Free to use online, or add it to your own app as an MIT-licensed plug-in.

Open the map, free → Get it on GitHub

What you can do

  • Drop in files

    KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, GPX, or a CSV with latitude and longitude columns.

  • Layer

    Show, hide, recolour and zoom to each file. Stack as many as you like.

  • Inspect

    Click any point, line or area to see its name and attributes.

  • Draw and measure

    Drop your own points and measure distances across the map.

  • Basemaps

    Switch between a street map and satellite imagery.

  • Export

    Save everything on the map as one GeoJSON file.

Your files stay with you

Carte reads your files in the browser. They're never uploaded, so sensitive locations stay on your machine. Only the map background is fetched from the internet.

Carte is new. Tell us what you'd like it to do.

In your own app

Carte is open source under the MIT licence. Add it to any web project as a plug-in, change it, ship it. The code and setup are on GitHub.
